The Anatomy of a Design Story

We believe luxury is not merely an aesthetic outcome; it is the rigor of process. Here is how MRMH navigates each commission from nascent brief to final heirloom reveal.

Case Study 01 • Ranchi Flagship Residence

The Morabadi Hillcrest Haven

A multigenerational residence balancing private intimacy with expansive hosting salon.

Area 3,400 sq.ft.
Timeline 14 Weeks (On Schedule)
Verified Investment ₹42L – ₹48L Bracket
Chapter 01

The Client Brief

A physician family moving back from Delhi desired an uncluttered, light-filled home that wouldn't feel sterile. Key constraint: existing structural RCC beams could not be relocated.

✓ Sound isolation between clinic & family rooms
✓ Low maintenance natural materials
Chapter 02

Spatial Concept

We embraced Japandi warmth with arched timber portals to camouflage the heavy structural columns. Re-oriented circulation to capture eastern morning light from Ranchi’s hill breeze.

✓ 3D Virtual VR Walkthrough alignment
✓ Micro-zoned acoustic ceiling coves
Chapter 03

Artisanal Execution

Turnkey carpentry crafted at our dedicated workshop with zero on-site sawdust disruption. Hand-selected bookmatched veneer flutes and champagne brass accents installed with millimeter precision.

✓ Zero tolerance joinery inspection
✓ Weekly video log updates to owners
Chapter 04

The Final Reveal

Handover completed 4 days prior to Diwali puja. Client noted: “The acoustics and warmth exceeded our dreams—every visitor comments on the effortless tranquility.”
